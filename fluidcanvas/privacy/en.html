1. Which app this policy covers

This policy covers the Android app FluidCanvas, formerly listed as PicselArt. The production package name is org.godotengine.picselart.

2. Information handled on your device

Drawing and simulation, device tilt, collision audio and optional vibration are processed on your device.

The current app does not provide accounts, advertising or analytics. There is no developer-operated service that sends drawings or sensor input off the device.

Preferences and the record that you dismissed the welcome hint are stored in app-private storage. Your drawing is a live simulation, not an automatically saved project that you can reopen later.

3. Saving a PNG

Choosing Save PNG opens Android's location selection screen (document picker). The app writes the image only to the destination you select. It does not use broad photo or storage access permissions.

If you select a cloud document provider as the destination, that service may synchronize or upload the image under its own policy.

4. Sharing a PNG

Choosing Share creates a temporary PNG in app-private cache and opens Android's sharing chooser. The receiving app you select is given read access to that image.

You choose whether and where to send it. Subsequent storage, transmission and deletion by the receiving app or service are governed by that app or service's features and policies.

Closing the sharing chooser does not confirm that the image was delivered.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Preferences: Settings persist until you change them or remove the app's local data. Restoring settings defaults intentionally preserves the record that you dismissed the welcome hint.
  • Sharing cache: Shared cached PNGs older than 24 hours are pruned the next time you share. This is not a scheduled deletion at the 24-hour mark. Android may clear the cache earlier.
  • Local app data: Clearing the app's storage (data) or uninstalling it removes its local settings and cache.
  • Saved or shared images: Clearing app data or uninstalling does not delete exported files or copies sent to other apps or services. To delete those, use the relevant destination or receiving app's controls.

6. Permissions

Optional haptics use Android's vibration permission.

Drawing does not need access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or your whole photo library or storage. Saving and sharing PNGs use the Android file-selection and sharing mechanisms described above.

7. Other services and this website

Data handling by Android, Google Play, your selected document provider and recipient apps is separate from the app's local processing described here. Please also review those services' privacy policies.

Hosting this policy page on GitHub Pages is separate from the app. According to GitHub's GitHub Pages documentation, visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details. This hosting-related processing is separate from the app's data processing.

This page does not include analytics, remotely loaded fonts or third-party scripts.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current app. If its features or data handling change, this page will be updated. An effective date will be stated when the policy is published or revised.
